Demographic change could increase pressure on the public finances

To isolate the effect of demographic change, we modelled a scenario in which the population changes while all other factors remain constant. A smaller workforce relative to the wider population would weaken tax receipts and increase pressure on public spending. In this scenario, the primary fiscal deficit widens from 1.4 per cent of gross domestic product today to 4.3 per cent in the early 2090s.

The implications extend beyond the public finances. The article considers how demographic change could affect labour supply, public services, consumer demand and the growing commercial importance of older audiences.
